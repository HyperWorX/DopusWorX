# Banner showcase

The image strip above is a frontmatter banner (`banner: banner.png` at the top
of this file). Two things to check, in both Reading and Live:

- It sits flush against the very top of the page card, with no gap above it.
- Its left and right edges fade out softly (an opacity fade) so it melts into
  the card instead of ending on a hard vertical line.

## Body

Everything below the banner is a normal document. Scroll and switch modes; the
banner stays pinned at the top of the card in both.

A paragraph of body text so the page has some content under the banner.
